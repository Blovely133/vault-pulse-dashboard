Format scorecard

Already matched

  • The spine. Hook → her résumé first → the silence → milestones-as-tells → injury test with a date/headline card → loyalty payoff → callback close. Beat for beat, in order.
  • "Her name is…" said in the first ~7 seconds — both videos.
  • Captions: same house style — white bold all-caps, per-word, bottom-center, no color emphasis on this lane.
  • Audio: VO narration over the same shared Cedar bed track used across the story lane.
  • Cutting grammar: hard-cut body, ~1 shot per 2.5–3.5s, dissolves saved for act seams and a chained-dissolve closing montage that mirrors the opening image.
  • Receipts pinned as cards while VO keeps rolling (Kyrie: ACL headline, birth-video card. Tatum: IG screenshot ×2, playoff stat bar, SEASON DEBUT chyron — Tatum actually leans harder here, and it's the v3 signature).
  • All-motion rule: both are ~95% real moving footage. No dead static stills — the only photo-derived shots are animated (Kyrie's ring/paparazzi zooms; Tatum's tunnel date-card hold).

Diverges — decide or accept

  • The hook phrasing. Kyrie opens on the question: "Do you know the story of…? Most fans don't." Tatum v3 opens declarative: "She never announced…" The v4 prepend drafts already exist (the "Do you know who Jayson Tatum goes home to?" option was the working pick) — this is the single highest-leverage change and it's blocked only on the hook line + story-lane VO take.
  • Length. 2:44 vs 1:29. Kyrie's extra length is almost all Act 6–7 (his own words + the why) — the part viewers stay for. Tatum has no equivalent confession footage, so matching runtime isn't free; it would need the March-comeback material stretched or the Paris family moment opened up.
  • Two-shots: 27% vs 0%. Kyrie lands every emotional peak on couple/family footage (source: Marlene's own 750K-sub vlog). Tatum/Ella have no couple catalog — the only public together-moment is Paris 2024 (family photo beat, currently told but not shown). Candidates if you want even one: the Paris clip, courtside broadcast shots of Ella at Celtics games, the Rubin July-4 photo.
  • Pace. v3 averages ~2.6s/shot vs Kyrie's ~3.3s and never holds longer than 5.3s (Kyrie holds 11.4s on the doula story). If v4 adds a confession-style hold, let it breathe like the Kyrie interview does.
  • Name cards. Tatum adds ELLA MAI lower-thirds (inherited from E! footage); Kyrie identifies by caption only. Harmless — keep.
