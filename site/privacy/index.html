Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026

Vault Pulse is a public channel analytics website that brings authorized YouTube and TikTok performance information into one dashboard. This policy explains what information the service uses and how it is handled.

Information we use

When a channel owner authorizes a platform connection, Vault Pulse may retrieve the account’s display name, profile image, public profile statistics, public video metadata, and public engagement statistics. The service does not request passwords or permission to publish, modify, or delete platform content.

Authentication data

Platform access and refresh tokens are kept in private, encrypted automation secrets. They are not included in the public website or its source data. Vault Pulse uses those credentials only to refresh the channel information that the account owner authorized.

How information is displayed

The dashboard publishes aggregated channel metrics and information about public posts. Because the website is publicly accessible, channel owners should connect only accounts whose performance information they are comfortable displaying.

Website visitors

Vault Pulse does not create visitor accounts, set advertising cookies, or sell visitor information. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard technical request information under GitHub’s own privacy terms.

Data retention and removal

The dashboard keeps a limited rolling analytics history. A connected channel owner may revoke Vault Pulse in the relevant platform’s account settings. After access is revoked, future refreshes stop. Requests to remove previously published dashboard data can be made through the project’s contact link below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the dashboard or its platform integrations change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or removal requests, contact the project owner through the Vault Pulse GitHub repository.
